## Brand & Style

The design system embraces an advanced, crystalline utility aesthetic rooted in Material 3 Expressive principles. Built for high-frequency media capture, parsing, and offline cataloging, the interface feels immediate, effortless, and engineered. It strips away standard utility clutter, trading industrial bulk for luminous arctic clarity, tactile pill containers, and ergonomic thumb-zone primacy.

### Personality & Demographics
- **Precision Engineering:** Crisp feedback, dynamic download telemetry, and instantaneous state transitions evoke confidence and technical mastery.
- **Cool & Calm Demeanor:** Pristine, light-washed surfaces paired with crystalline cyan and deep oceanic indigos eliminate sensory fatigue during long archive reviews.
- **Ergonomic Speed:** Deep visual hierarchy designed specifically for one-handed operational efficiency on modern edge-to-edge mobile screens.

### Design Movement
**Material 3 Hyper-Clean with Tactile Tonal Layers.** The visual language rejects pure flat geometry in favor of tiered, squircle-contoured tonal surfaces, pill micro-badges, and selective frosted blurs on sticky system sheets.

## Colors

The palette balances icy brightness with deep oceanic legibility. High-luminance cyan anchors primary intent without washing out, while rich indigo stabilizes text hierarchies and dark structural accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#00687A` - Arctic Cyan Deep):** Main interactive driver. Anchors key download actions, active pill indicators, filled radio controls, and track scrubbing thumbs.
- **Secondary (`#1B365D` - Abyssal Indigo):** Grounding balance. Applied to top-tier structural titles, floating controls, pinned filters, and high-emphasis micro-states.
- **Tertiary (`#00A896` - Glacier Teal):** Precision metric indicator. Governs download velocity tags, bitrate pills, audio format selectors, and complete states.
- **Neutral (`#5A6065` - Slate Glaze):** Structural midtones and muted supporting details.

### Surface System (Light Mode Tiering)
- **Surface (`#F6FAFB`):** Base canvas background. Neutral light tone with a faint arctic cast.
- **Surface Dim (`#E8ECEE`):** Distinct recessed zones, background track troughs, and disabled controls.
- **Surface Container Low (`#F0F4F6`):** Passive cards, inactive sheet drawers, and queue cards at rest.
- **Surface Container (`#EAEEF0`):** Default card background, segment switches, and search input fields.
- **Surface Container High (`#E2E6E9`):** Elevated modals, drag handles, and interactive overlay menus.
- **Surface Container Highest (`#D9DDE0`):** Floating action pods and unselected chip borders.

### Semantic Accents
- **Success (`#007F5F`):** Complete downloads and validated URL parsers.
- **Warning (`#C97A00`):** Format stream degradation and battery throttling indicators.
- **Error (`#BA1A1A`):** Network timeout, restricted streams, and corrupt stream alerts.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy utilizes **Inter** across all display, body, and telemetry levels to reinforce an engineered, metric-driven personality with strict legibility at small scale.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Format & Speed Readouts (`label-sm`, `label-md`):** Fixed to uppercase tabular styling where applicable, delivering stable frame counters, megabyte tallies, and transfer rate numbers without layout jitter.
- **Media Headings (`headline-md`, `title-lg`):** Clamped with strict two-line truncation and tight tracking (`-0.1px` to `-0.2px`) for dense video titles.
- **Metadata Subtitles (`body-sm`):** Rendered in neutral midtones to maintain a 2:1 visual ratio against card headers.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to an 8dp grid rhythm (with a 4dp micro-step), tuned for Android 14 edge-to-edge system standards and dynamic gesture navigation bars.

### Grid System
- **Mobile (Compact, &lt;600dp):** 4-column fluid layout with `16dp` outer margins and `16dp` gutters. All primary action hubs anchor within the lower 40% vertical viewport for thumb comfort.
- **Tablet (Medium, 600–840dp):** 8-column layout with `32dp` margins and `24dp` gutters. Media queues reflow into a multi-column dynamic grid.
- **Desktop / Foldable Expanded (&gt;840dp):** 12-column layout with split-pane master-detail viewports (parsing dock on the left, download matrix on the right).

### Safe Areas & Insets
Top app bars enforce an explicit clearance of `status_bar_height + space-sm`. Floating bottom bars sit `space-md` above the system navigational indicator line to eliminate tap interception.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Layering** supplemented by **Ambient Colored Shadows**, rejecting heavy dark drops in favor of soft, tinted ambient diffusions.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Base canvas background (`#F6FAFB`). No shadow, no outline.
- **Level 1 (Docked Containers):** Media download cards, clipboard parsing sheets at rest. Uses `Surface Container Low` with a 1px border of `rgba(0, 104, 122, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements):** Segmented buttons, quick filter bars, and card hover states. Box shadow: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.06), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Bars & Modals):** Primary FABs, dynamic parsing bottom sheets, toast notifications. Box shadow: `0px 10px 30px -4px rgba(0, 104, 122, 0.16), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.08)`.
- **Level 4 (Overlay Dialogs):** Format conversion configuration sheets, permission dialogs. Box shadow: `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.22)`.

### Blur Application
Sticky system headers and bottom control rails use backdrop filter blurs (`blur(20px)`) coupled with 85% opacity surface containers for seamless background scroll-through.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is unified around squircle pill radii that echo smooth stone hardware forms and modern Android widget architecture.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Micro Radii (`rounded-md`, 8dp):** Selection tags, bitrate badges, and video duration overlays.
- **Component Radii (`rounded-lg`, 16dp):** Embedded input fields, stream option tiles, and audio preview containers.
- **Container Radii (`rounded-xl`, 24dp):** Media queue cards, bottom sheets, alert frames, and thumbnail preview crops.
- **Full Radii (`rounded-full`, 9999dp):** Floating action buttons, segmented filter pills, status micro-chips, and search action bars.

## Components

### Action Buttons
- **Primary Pill Action (Download / Start):** High-prominence pill with 56dp height on mobile. Filled with Primary (`#00687A`), text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`), bold icon prefix (24dp), and `rounded-full` boundaries. Hover states lighten by 8%; pressed states compress with a scale factor of `0.97` alongside an internal ink ripple.
- **Secondary Tonal Action:** Surface Container High fill, Abyssal Indigo text, no stroke. Used for queue batch actions and format reconfiguration.
- **Floating Download Hub (FAB):** 64dp squircle pill centered at the bottom viewport edge. Casts an arctic-tinted Level 3 shadow with an embedded download counter micro-badge.

### Chips & Badges
- **Format Pills (4K, 1080p, MP3, OPUS):** Height 28dp, `rounded-full`, padding `0 12dp`. Inactive: Surface Container with Slate Glaze text. Active: Tertiary (`#00A896`) background with white text and a leading check indicator.
- **Telemetry Badges (Speed & Size):** Compact 20dp height, `label-sm` font, monospaced tabular numerals. Surface Dim background with high-contrast neutral text.

### Input Fields (Smart URL Extraction Dock)
- **Bar Anatomy:** Full-width pill container (`rounded-full`, 56dp height) rendered in `Surface Container`. Zero external borders; active state displays a crisp 2dp focus ring in Primary Cyan.
- **Clipboard Quick-Action Button:** Integrated into the trailing edge of the field as an autonomous pill chip labeled "Paste" with high tactile feedback.

### Media Download Cards
- **Card Structure:** 24dp squircle container (`Surface Container Low`), containing a 16:9 thumbnail preview (`rounded-lg`), title block, format pill cluster, and an inline progress track.
- **Active Progress Ribbon:** 6dp height continuous rounded track. Trough is `Surface Dim`; indicator uses a primary-to-tertiary gradient bar paired with an animated pulse cap.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 24dp bounds with a minimum 48dp tactile touch target. Selected radio controls display an 8dp solid Arctic Cyan dot enclosed in a 2dp border. Checkboxes transition from flat border rings into solid filled cyan squares with pure white iconography.
- **Segmented Converters (Audio / Video Toggle):** Enclosed in an integrated 48dp capsule track. Active sliding tab utilizes Level 2 elevation and crisp white contrast.
